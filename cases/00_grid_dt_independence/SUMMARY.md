## Metric definitions

- `W = sqrt(Fpx^2 + Fpy^2)` and the force angle `atan2(Fpy, Fpx)` are read from the
  `Fp` vector array of the final flat VTK (the solver fills these as uniform fields;
  they are not columns of `diagnostics.csv`, contrary to the README wording).
- `p_max` is the largest cell-centre value of `p` in the final flat VTK.
- Cavitation area fraction counts cells with `film_content < 0.999`; the mesh is
  uniform, so the cell-count fraction is the area fraction.
- THD temperatures are cell-centre extrema and the arithmetic cell mean of `T`.
- 00b eccentricity ratio is `sqrt(xB^2 + yB^2) / c` from the `xB` array; settling
  time is the first VTK output time after which `|e(t) - e_final| < 0.01` holds for
  the remainder of the record.

### Notes on 00a

- `grid_G1` (60 x 16) is clearly off the trend: W is 1.3% below G2 and the force
  angle is 2.6 deg from the G5 value. G2 onward the load is stable to better than
  0.06% per refinement.

  not.** `p_max` falls monotonically by 1.1-1.6% at every refinement with no sign of
  levelling off, and the location of the peak cell is still migrating
  (theta = 249.0, 247.5, 248.3, 246.5, 245.6 deg and z = 0.0375 -> 0.0397 m for
  G1..G5, against the L/2 = 0.04 m mid-plane). The mean pressure, by contrast,
  converges cleanly (0.7209 -> 0.7369 MPa over G1..G5). This is the usual behaviour
  of a point extremum sampled at cell centres near a sharp pressure peak.
- No run hit `max_outer_iters` (worst 9 of 300). No run stopped on non-positive
  film. `converged = 1` on every diagnostics row.
- Mass balance is clean for every grid run: `liquid_mass` is identical between the
  first and last diagnostics row (drift exactly 0, these are single-step steady
  solves) and the worst normalised liquid residual is 6.9e-7 (G4).

Notes on the THD pair:

1. **The committed `config.txt` in both THD directories fails validation and cannot
   run.** With `fluid_property_model = SINGLE_PHASE` the solver requires
   `dissolved_gas_henry_coeff > 0` and a non-zero `solution_viscosity_gas_coeff`,
   but both are 0 in the committed files, so the run aborts with
   "Configuration failed validation". The originals were left untouched; a
   `config_thd_fixed.txt` sits beside each, identical except for those two keys set
   to `1e-12`. Both are inert here (`dissolved_gas_initial = 0`, gas transport is a
   no-op outside `TWO_PHASE`, and the viscosity term is `exp(a_c * c_g)` with
   `c_g = 0`), so the physics is unchanged. **This needs a real fix in the case
   configs or in the validator before the campaign proceeds.**
2. **These runs are not exercising thermo-viscous coupling.** The energy equation is
   active and produces a large temperature rise (313.15 K initial -> 374-379 K), but
   W changes by only 0.006% and the friction torque by 0.004% against the isothermal
   runs on the same grid. The reason is the viscosity law: the solver implements
   Andrade, `mu = mu_ref * exp[E_mu (1/T - 1/T_ref)]`, so
   `viscosity_temperature_coeff` has units of K; the configs set it to 0.034. For a
   65 K rise that gives `exp(0.034 * -5.6e-4) = 1 - 1.9e-5`, i.e. no effect. A value
   of 0.034 is the standard *linear* coefficient `beta` in 1/K for
   `mu = mu_0 exp(-beta dT)`; the Andrade equivalent for an ISO VG 32 oil is of
   order 3000-4500 K. As configured, the THD runs are energy-equation-only and give
   no independent grid check of the coupled problem. Worth resolving before the
   Ferron comparison.
3. Grid behaviour of the THD pair mirrors the isothermal pair exactly (p_max falls
   1.54% from G3_thd to G4_thd), and the temperature field itself is essentially
   grid independent: T_max changes by 0.053 K (0.014%) and T_mean by 0.050 K
   (0.013%) between the two grids.

Observations (all superseded):

- Every run reaches a genuine force balance: the pressure resultant settles at
  6024.9 / 6024.9 / 6026.7 N against the 6000 N applied load (0.4% high, worth a
  separate look at the force integration, but consistent across dt).
- The trajectory is **damped oscillatory** in all three: the bearing overshoots to
  e ~ 0.476-0.486 near t = 0.010 s, undershoots near t = 0.025-0.030 s, then settles.
  It is not monotone.
- Settling time is 0.020 s in all three, but this metric is blunt here: the
  configured `write_interval` of 0.005 s gives only 4 samples inside the settling
  window, so it cannot resolve differences between dt. If the redone sweep is to
  use settling time as a criterion, `write_interval` should be reduced to about
  5e-4 s.
- **Equilibrium eccentricity is not dt-converged over the range tested, and it is
  moving the wrong way.** The change from T1 to T2 is +0.099%, but from T2 to T3 it
  is +0.713% - larger, not smaller. The overshoot grows monotonically with
  refinement (0.0411 -> 0.0451 -> 0.0476) and the equilibrium attitude angle drifts
  monotonically (144.735 -> 143.972 -> 143.641 deg). With T4 and T5 missing there is
  no way to tell whether this is heading somewhere; the sequence as it stands does
  not demonstrate timestep independence.
- No dt run hit `max_outer_iters` (worst 7 of 50) and none stopped on non-positive
  film. Worst normalised liquid residual 5.1e-6 (T1), improving with dt
  (2.6e-6 at T2, 1.1e-6 at T3).
- `liquid_mass` falls about 9.7% from the first to the last diagnostics row in each
  run. **This is physical, not a conservation defect**: the bearing moves from
  e = 0.1 to e ~ 0.44, and the cavitated fraction rises from near zero to 0.53-0.55,
  so the resident liquid mass genuinely drops. The per-step normalised residual
  above is the conservation measure, and it is small.

## Flags for the user

1. `p_max` is not grid independent on G1-G5: 1.1-1.6% change per level, apparent
   order 0.137, fine-grid GCI 1.77% even at an assumed p = 2. W, torque and
   cavitation fraction are converged. (README guidance is <1% for both W and p_max.)
2. Both THD `config.txt` files fail solver validation as committed and were run from
   a `config_thd_fixed.txt` variant; see the 00a notes.
3. `viscosity_temperature_coeff = 0.034` is being consumed as an Andrade coefficient
   in K, which makes it inert. The THD runs therefore do not test thermo-viscous
   coupling.
4. The 00b sweep was stopped and is superseded; on the G3 grid it had not
   demonstrated dt independence (change in equilibrium eccentricity grew from
   +0.099% to +0.713% with refinement).
5. `write_interval = 0.005` s is too coarse to resolve settling time for the dt
   study; consider 5e-4 s when the sweep is redone.
6. The equilibrium pressure resultant is consistently ~0.4% above the 6000 N applied
   load in every dt run.
7. No run hit `max_outer_iters` and no run stopped on non-positive film.
