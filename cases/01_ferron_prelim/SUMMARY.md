# Case 01 - Ferron (1983) Fig. 6 preliminary, isothermal

Purpose: place the solver at the Ferron 4000 rpm / 6 kN operating point without
any thermal physics, and see how far an isothermal film gets on its own. The two
viscosities bracket the missing THD effect: 0.0277 Pa s is the 40 C supply
value, 0.0166 Pa s is a rough 55 C effective value. THD closes the gap later.

Common to every run (base: `cases/00_grid_dt_independence/grid_G6_fbns/config.txt`):

## Sign convention (determined empirically, not assumed)

The moving-bearing reference run `cases/00_grid_dt_independence/dt_T1_fbns`
carries `external_load_y = +6000`. Its latest written frame (t = 0.01352 s, run
still in progress and still transient) reports a pressure-force resultant

    Fp = (-1001.1, -3046.1) N,  |Fp| = 3206 N,  angle = -108.19 deg

i.e. the pressure force sits in the **-y** half plane while the bearing is being
pushed in +y. Equilibrium requires `Fp = -F_ext`, so the STATIC target here is

    Fp_target = (0, -6000) N  --  magnitude 6000 N, direction -90 deg.

## Force-balance search

Damped Newton on (eps, attitude_angle_deg) with a forward-difference Jacobian
(`force_balance.py`). Every solver call, including the Jacobian probes, is
archived as `fb_iter_NN/` with its config and `results/`; `fb_log.json` maps each
iterate to (mu, eps, psi, forces). Tolerance: |W| to 0.1 % and force direction to
0.05 deg. Both viscosities converged in 3 Newton steps (7 solver calls each,
about 11 s per call).

| iterate map | mu40 | mu55 |
|---|---|---|
| start x0 | `fb_iter_00` (eps 0.500, psi -45.0 deg) | `fb_iter_07` (eps 0.620, psi -40.0 deg) |
| Jacobian probes | `fb_iter_01`, `02`, `04`, `05` | `fb_iter_08`, `09`, `11`, `12` |
| Newton steps | `fb_iter_03`, `06` | `fb_iter_10`, `13` |
| converged | **`fb_iter_06`** | **`fb_iter_13`** |

## Equilibrium

| quantity | mu40 (0.0277 Pa s) | mu55 (0.0166 Pa s) |
|---|---|---|
| eccentricity ratio eps = e/c | **0.48166** | **0.60638** |
| `attitude_angle_deg` (min-film line, solver input) | **-31.278 deg** | **-39.453 deg** |
| classical attitude angle phi = psi - angle(Fp) | 58.72 deg | 50.55 deg |
| eccentricity e | 7.3212e-5 m | 9.2170e-5 m |
| Fp | (0.589, -5999.56) N | (0.150, -6000.31) N |
| **W check** | 5999.56 N (-0.0073 % of 6000) | 6000.31 N (+0.0052 % of 6000) |
| force direction | -89.9944 deg (0.0056 deg off target) | -89.9986 deg (0.0014 deg off target) |
| p_max (absolute) | 1.8381 MPa | 2.0563 MPa |
| p_max (gauge) | 1.7368 MPa | 1.9549 MPa |
| h_min | 78.79 um | 59.83 um |
| cavitated area fraction | 0.248 | 0.273 |
| friction torque | 5.6567 N m | 3.8262 N m |

Both are inside the requested tolerances (0.1 % on |W|, 0.05 deg on direction).
The classical attitude angles are consistent with plain-bearing expectations at
these eccentricities, an independent check that the (eps, psi) pair is physical
and not just a numerical root.

## Mid-plane pressure vs experiment

Figure: `ferron_midplane_pressure.png` (single 300 dpi PNG, lab style,
generated by `plot_ferron.py`).

**Caption / mapping assumption.** Mid-span film pressure at the force-balanced
equilibrium for both viscosities, against the digitised Ferron et al. (1983)
Fig. 6 experiment at 4000 rpm and 6 kN. The experimental abscissa is degrees
from the supply-groove CENTRE in the direction of rotation; the solver groove
centre is at theta = 90 deg and rotation is +theta, so the experiment is placed
at solver_theta = (90 + x) mod 360 and both curves are drawn against
(theta - 90) mod 360. This is the stated mapping applied as-is: no shift was
fitted or adjusted to improve agreement. The theta origin of the digitisation is
flagged as unconfirmed against the PDF in the CSV header (the paper's
nomenclature defines theta from maximum film thickness); if that reading is
wrong, every experimental point moves by the measured attitude angle. Pressure
is gauge, i.e. solver absolute pressure minus 101325 Pa.

Peak comparison:

| | peak gauge pressure | peak location (deg from groove) |
|---|---|---|
| experiment | 1.828 MPa | 202.3 |
| mu = 0.0277 | 1.737 MPa (-5.0 %) | 199.25 (-3.0) |
| mu = 0.0166 | 1.955 MPa (+6.9 %) | 199.75 (-2.5) |

Qualitative reading:

- The peak LOCATION is reproduced to within about 3 deg by both viscosities and
  is essentially viscosity-insensitive. That is the strongest evidence that the
  assumed theta mapping is the right one.
- The peak MAGNITUDE is bracketed as intended: 40 C under-predicts by 5 %, 55 C
  over-predicts by 7 %, and the experiment sits between them. That is what a
  missing THD viscosity drop should look like.
- The rising flank (0-180 deg) is followed closely by both curves; the 40 C curve
  tracks the data slightly better there, the 55 C curve is better at the crest.
- The falling flank is the visible discrepancy. The solver ruptures the film and
  drops to p_cav near 250-260 deg, whereas the experiment still reads about
  0.24 MPa at 233 deg and only reaches zero past 270 deg. The isothermal FBNS
  film cavitates slightly early and too sharply; the measured decay is more
  gradual.
- Over the cavitated arc (270-360 deg) both solver curves sit at
  p_cav = 1.0e5 Pa, i.e. -1.3 kPa gauge, against a measured ~0 gauge. That
  offset is the chosen p_cav, not an error.

## Files

- `config_template.txt` - parametrised config (e, attitude, mu).
- `solver_io.py` - run driver and VTK/pvts readers (shared with case 02).
- `force_balance.py` - the 2D Newton driver.
- `fb_log.json`, `equilibrium.json` - every iterate, and the two converged points.
- `fb_iter_00 .. fb_iter_13` - one archived run per solver call.
- `probe/` - the initial orientation run (eps 0.60, psi -90 deg, mu40).
- `plot_ferron.py`, `ferron_midplane_pressure.png` - the deliverable figure.
- `progress.log` - running record.
